# ScrapBro Privacy Policy

ScrapBro does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.

- **Captured pages.** Pages you capture, together with their URLs and titles, are stored only on your own computer,
  in the scrapbook folder you choose, through the locally installed ScrapBro backend.
- **Settings.** Your settings are kept in the browser's own extension storage on your computer. They are not synced
  or sent anywhere.
- **The backend.** The backend program runs on your computer and only accepts connections from the same computer
  (127.0.0.1). It does not connect to the internet.
- **Network access.** The extension contacts github.com only to download the backend from the project's official
  releases, when you ask for it on the settings page. When you capture a page, its images, styles and other
  resources are loaded from the website you are capturing, as the browser would when showing that page.
- **No tracking.** ScrapBro has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party tracking.

Links on the settings page (such as the donation page) open only when you click them.

Questions: https://github.com/unalignedcoder/scrapbro/issues
